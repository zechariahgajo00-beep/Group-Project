Guides On How To Use AI Properly




When using AI we should always remember to use it for aiding in our study and not for answering it. Avoid constantly using AI with every problem you face and remember that AI is a tool not a being that will grant you what you want, so only use AI to aid you, not to do things for you. According to Frontiers relying on ai can lead to reduced self-regulation, and emotional dependence on AI tools, concerns include weaker independent engagement, academic integrity risks, and distress when ai is unavailable.
