About

Many students have relied too much on AI nowadays, so we created this to teach the students on how to properly use Ai. We shouldn't use Ai to answer our activities for us, instead use it as a study aid not a shortcut. For example, using ai to answer quizzes, activities, assignments and other school works, how are we supposed to learn if we rely on ai to do the things for us. Additionally, according to Berkeley News, among 95,000 undergraduates 9% admitted to using ai to cheat and almost 40% used it monthly or even more frequently.
