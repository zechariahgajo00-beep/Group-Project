Responsible Use Of Generative Ai In Education




Our topic is all about the responsible use of AI in education. In this generation most students have relied too much on ai to do their tasks for them, so we made this to teach the students on how to use ai responsibly. Our objective is for students to understand the correct and proper way to use ai without relying on it with every tasks given to them.




"Use AI as a study aid, not a shortcut"
